# HookChop

**Your best moments, clipped by AI for Shorts and Reels**

Website: https://hookchop.com

HookChop uses AI to find the best moments in your long videos and turn them into captioned vertical clips, ready for Shorts, Reels and TikTok.

HookChop is an AI clip maker built for creators, podcasters and marketers. Upload a long video, podcast or stream and HookChop finds the moments worth sharing, then cuts them into short vertical clips with accurate captions — ready to post on YouTube Shorts, Instagram Reels and TikTok. No timeline scrubbing, no manual editing: the AI handles clip selection, cropping and captioning so one long recording becomes a week of short-form content. Start free with 60 minutes a month, no credit card needed.

## Features

- AI finds the most engaging moments automatically (clip scoring 0-100)
- Accurate word-level auto-captions in 11 styles
- Silence and filler-word removal
- Vertical 9:16 formatting (also 4:5, 1:1, 16:9 MP4 export)
- Publish/schedule directly to YouTube Shorts, Instagram Reels, Facebook Reels
- Works with videos, podcasts and live streams
- Works with Claude, OpenAI, Gemini and local AI models
